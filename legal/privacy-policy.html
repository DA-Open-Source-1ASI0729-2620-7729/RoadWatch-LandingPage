Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, contractual obligations, security requirements, and applicable legal or regulatory duties.

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of their personal information. Requests can be sent to support@roadwatch.com.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact RoadWatch at support@roadwatch.com.
